# Acme Deals: on-brand ads

> Source: `example-output/campaign.html` in Nick Christensen's [ship-icp-ads-automate-monitoring](https://github.com/nickyc1/ship-icp-ads-automate-monitoring) (MIT, see `raw/voc/example/LICENSE`). A Google Performance Max asset group for the "Agency Jordan" avatar, every line scored 4/5 or 5/5 against the ICP. Copied verbatim; the scores are Nick's. Audience signals and the scorer's rejected lines are left out: they are targeting and off-brand respectively.

- **Page focus:** Lifetime deals for agencies
- **Target ICP:** Agency Jordan (solo / small agency owner)
- **Job to be done:** Turn one-time tool buyers into resellers who bill clients monthly
