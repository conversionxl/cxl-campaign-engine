# Acme Deals example campaign data

Fictional. Acme Deals is a lifetime-deal marketplace whose best customers are small marketing agencies that buy tools once and resell them into client retainers (see `brand-brain/icp.md`). In 2026 Acme launched **Acme for Agencies**, a $1,200-a-year plan with multi-client licensing and white-label rights, sold with a 20-minute demo. That is what the example campaigns sell.

| File | What it is | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `brand-brain/` | The four brain files from the Marketing Brain exercises run on Acme | every command in `example` mode, in place of `wiki/brand/` |
| `past-briefs/2026-q2-agency-plan-launch.md` | The small-c brief Acme ran in Q2 2026, as the team wrote it then | `/campaign-brief example`: what to keep, what to fix |
| `past-briefs/2026-h2-agency-category-plan.md` | The half-year plan the Q2 brief was phase 1 of | the big-C demo |
| `results/2026-q2-agency-plan-results.md` | What the Q2 campaign returned, by channel | goal baselines, the lesson |
| `sales-feedback-2026-q2.md` | Notes from the two account executives on the Q2 leads | section 7, the sales enablement workflow |
| `live/crm-snapshot-2026-09-30.md` | Pipeline, deal size and stage conversion rates | pipeline math, realistic targets |
| `live/email-performance-2026.md` | Every email Acme sent agencies this year, with open, click and reply rates | the email workflows, subject line evidence |
| `calendar-2026-q4.md` | Launches, events and blackout dates for the rest of the year | section 9 |

Names and companies map to `../../voc/example/customers.csv`. No email addresses appear outside that CSV.
