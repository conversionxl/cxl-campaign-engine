## Stage conversion, agency segment, 2026 year to date

| Stage | Count | Conversion to next stage |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified lead (agency, 1 to 20 people, 3+ clients, demo request or licensing question) | 164 | 48% to meeting held |
| Meeting held | 79 | 52% to opportunity |
| Opportunity (proposal sent) | 41 | 56% to closed won |
| Closed won | 23 | |

Rough rule the team uses: **1 in 7 qualified leads buys; 1 in 3.5 meetings held buys.**

Sales cycle, qualified lead to closed won: median 19 days. Meetings booked within 2 days of the lead's action convert to opportunity at 61%; after 5 days, 34%.
