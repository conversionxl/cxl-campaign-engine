# Email performance: agency sends, 2026

> Fictional example of a live pull. Tool: Acme's email platform. Report: all campaigns and automations sent to the agency segment (4,310 contacts), 1 January to 30 September 2026. Pulled 30 September 2026. Aggregates only.

## What moves the agency segment, from this data

- Subject lines that name a specific tool count, a customer's situation, or answer a question outperform announcements by 1.5 to 2x on open and 2 to 3x on click.
- Replies, not clicks, predicted demos in Q2. Every reply with a licensing question was a qualified lead.
- The Q2 reminders fell off send over send; nothing new was said.
- No sequence has ever made a demo ask directly. The 3 Sep send is the closest, and it produced 14 demo requests from one email.
