# Acme for Agencies: H2 2026 plan

> Fictional example of a big-C plan, as the Acme leadership team wrote it in June 2026. The Q2 launch brief was phase 1. Several sections were left as placeholders, which is normal and is what the engine can draft from the rest. Source: Acme leadership deck, "Agencies H2 plan", June 2026.

## Objective

Make Acme the default way small agencies buy software: own the "lifetime deals for agencies" category by the end of 2026.

## Lagging goal

$120,000 in Agencies plan revenue in H2 2026 (about 110 plans at $1,080).

## Pipeline math (as written in June)

- Gap: $120,000. ACV: $1,080. Plans needed: 111.
- At 1 in 3.5 meetings held: 390 meetings held.
- At 48% lead to meeting: 810 qualified leads.
- Coverage ratio: 3x on opportunities.

Note from Dana: these numbers assume the Q2 conversion rates hold. They did not; see the Q2 readout.

## TAM check

IF there are about 25,000 small agencies in our four markets with 3 or more clients AND we can reach 10% of them in six months at a 3% qualified-lead rate THEN we get 75 qualified leads a month, which is short of the 135 a month the math needs. Conclusion in June: the plan is reachable only with the existing-customer upgrade pool (612 agencies on lifetime deals), not from cold reach alone.

## Journey map

| Stage | The buyer's thought | Content | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem aware | "Every tool I don't pay monthly for is one less thing draining the account" | Margin calculator, margin blog posts | Marketing |
| Solution aware | "Can I use one purchase across all my client accounts?" | Licensing explainer, white-label vendor list | Marketing + Legal |
| Product aware | "Why pay $1,200 a year on top of the deals I own?" | Customer stories with per-client math, demo | Sales |
| Most aware | "I need this before the price goes up" | Price-rise notice, renewal offers | Sales + Account management |
