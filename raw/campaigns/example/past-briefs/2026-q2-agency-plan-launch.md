# Campaign brief: Acme for Agencies launch (Q2 2026)

> Fictional example. Written by the Acme marketing team in April 2026, before the Campaign Engine. Kept as they wrote it, mixed copy and all, so the engine has something real-shaped to learn from. Source: Acme marketing shared drive, "Q2 Agency Plan Launch Brief v3".

**Owner:** Dana (Head of Marketing)
**Dates:** 14 April to 30 June 2026
**Budget:** $6,000 paid, plus list sends

## Goals

- Launch Acme for Agencies ($1,200/yr) to our agency customers and lookalikes
- Drive awareness of the new plan
- Book demos with agency owners
- Target: 400 downloads of the "Agency Margin Calculator", 40 demos, 12 closed plans

## Audience

Agency owners and consultants. Also in-house marketers who might want multi-seat.

## Key messages

- Buy once, bill clients monthly. Now with multi-client licensing built in.
- White-label every tool in your stack.
- Unlock agency growth without the overhead.
- Trusted by 2,000+ agencies (check this number with Sam before launch)

## Offer

The Agency Margin Calculator: a Google Sheet that shows how much margin you keep by reselling a lifetime tool into retainers vs paying monthly per seat. Gated behind email + company + role + company size + phone + "how many clients do you serve".

## Channels

- LinkedIn ads (single image, 6 variants) to agency owner job titles, US/UK/DE/CA
- Email to the full list (18,400 contacts), 3 sends
- Blog post: "The agency cheat code: how solo agencies run 10 clients"
- Landing page /agencies
- Sales follow-up by Priya and Marcus (AEs) on every download
