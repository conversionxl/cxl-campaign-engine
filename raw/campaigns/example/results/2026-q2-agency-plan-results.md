# Results: Acme for Agencies launch (Q2 2026)

> Fictional example. Pulled by Dana on 3 July 2026 from LinkedIn Campaign Manager, the email tool and the CRM. Source: Acme marketing shared drive, "Q2 launch readout".

**Campaign:** 14 April to 30 June 2026. Budget spent: $5,840 paid.

517 downloads, 9 demos. Downloads beat target; the business goal missed by three quarters.

## By channel

In-house marketers were a third of downloads and none of the demos. The brief had them in the audience "in case they want multi-seat".

## The path from download to demo

- 517 downloads. Every one went to Priya and Marcus as a CRM task with no context beyond name and company.
- Median time to first call: 6 working days. 214 downloads were never called.
- The only follow-up email was the automated "here is your calculator" email. No sequence.
- Demo requests came almost entirely from downloads who replied to that single email asking a licensing question (7 of 9).

## What the team concluded (readout, 3 July)

- "We sold the calculator, not the plan." The CTA everywhere was the download; the demo ask only appeared on the thank-you page, in small text.
- "In-house people downloaded a spreadsheet about agency margin. Of course they did not book a demo."
- "Six fields plus phone killed form conversion from ads (page conversion 9%) and still did not give sales anything useful."
- "The 'unlock' and 'scale' variants are not how our customers talk." Dana pointed at the voice guide.
- "Legal pulled 'white-label every tool' in week 2; the landing page said it for two weeks."
- Next time: one KPI (demos), exclude in-house, call within a day, a real sequence with the demo ask in it, and proof we can stand behind.
