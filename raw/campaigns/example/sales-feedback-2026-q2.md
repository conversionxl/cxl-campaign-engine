# Sales feedback on Q2 campaign leads

> Fictional example. Notes from the 8 July 2026 marketing and sales retro, typed up by Dana. Priya and Marcus are Acme's two account executives for the Agencies plan. Names of leads map to `../../voc/example/customers.csv` where they are customers.

## Priya (AE, US and CA)

- "I got 260 tasks in a quarter that said 'downloaded calculator'. No company size, no client count, nothing about what they saw. I opened the CRM record and had to google half of them."
- "The ones who converted had already asked us a licensing question. Marisol Vega replied to the delivery email asking whether one plan covers all her client accounts. That is a buyer. I want those flagged, not buried in the same list as students."
- "When I called within a day or two, people remembered the calculator and we talked. After a week they thought I was cold calling."
- "Give me three things before I dial: what they downloaded, what their number came out at in the calculator if we can capture it, and the one angle to open with. I will write my own opener, I just need the context."
- "Half the in-house folks were polite and confused. 'I thought this was a free tool.' We wasted their time and ours."

## Marcus (AE, UK and EU)

- "The talk track I used was the one from the 2024 lifetime deal launch. It does not mention multi-client licensing at all. I made it up as I went."
- "Objection I heard most: 'Which tools actually allow white-label? Your page says all of them.' After legal changed the page I had nothing to point to. Give me the list of vendors that allow it and I can close those conversations."
- "Second objection: 'I already own 30 deals, why would I pay $1,200 a year on top?' I needed the margin math per client, not per tool. Tomas Becker did the math himself on the call and bought."
- "Please stop sending me downloads with a university email domain."
- "Meetings that came from the blog post were the best prepared. They had read 1,500 words about the model before we talked. Two out of two closed."

## What sales asked marketing for

1. One lead definition, agreed before launch: agency or consultancy, 1 to 20 people, client count 3 or more, and a demo request or a licensing question. Everything else goes to nurture, not to an AE.
2. An alert within the hour that names the campaign, what the person did and the opener to use.
3. A one-page briefing per campaign, and a talk track that mentions the actual offer.
4. The vendor list that permits white-label, as a document reps can send.
5. Call every qualified lead within one working day. Marketing measures it.
