# raw/performance/

Evidence of what works: analytics and performance data for the pages, messages, and content the brand already runs. Exercises 2 and 3 read it.

**Stays on your machine.** `.gitignore` excludes everything here except this README.

## What goes here

- GA4 or Search Console exports: sessions, engagement, and conversion per landing page or post
- Lead-gen and conversion reports: leads and closed deals by landing page, source, or campaign
- Order data by landing page or UTM
- Ad, email, and social performance: CTR, conversion, engagement per message or post

Commands save live pulls from connected tools here as dated snapshots, with the tool, the report or query, and the date range at the top. You can also drop exports in, or paste them into the chat.

How the exercises use it: `frameworks/live-data-and-research.md`.
