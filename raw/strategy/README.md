# raw/strategy/

Where the business is going, and the internal docs that say so. All three exercises read this folder for direction; the ICP never treats it as evidence of who buys.

**Stays on your machine.** `.gitignore` excludes everything here except this README.

## What goes here

- Strategy docs, plans for a pivot, a new market, product, or price
- Board or leadership decks that set direction
- Internal positioning and messaging docs, messaging houses, sales narratives
- Brand books or voice guides that are internal or under NDA

Drop files in, paste them into the chat while a command runs (it files them here), or name a doc in a connected tool (Google Drive, Notion, ClickUp, Asana, Confluence) for the command to fetch. Label each with its source and date.

How the exercises use it: `frameworks/live-data-and-research.md`.
